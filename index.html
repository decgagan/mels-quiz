Mel's Quiz

Win as much money as you can! Before each question, pick Easy, Medium or Hard. The harder the question, the bigger the prize.

Playing as

Make a profile to save your scores and get on the leaderboard.

How to play
  • You have 30 seconds for each question.
  • You have 3 lives ♥♥♥. A wrong answer, or running out of time, costs a life. The game goes on until they're all gone, and you keep what you've won.
  • Every 10th question is a big hard one, worth double.
  • Six lifelines, from a Hint to Double or nothing, cost a little money, and you can use each one once per game.

📅 Weekly challenge

Three topics, ten questions each. You get one go at each topic. Wrong answers cost you money but don't end your go. Every go goes on one weekly top 10 for all three topics, so everyone has a good chance of making it. The board starts afresh every Monday.

You need a profile to play, so we know whose go it is.
